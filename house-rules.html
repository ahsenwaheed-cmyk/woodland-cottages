Before you arrive

House Rules

A short list, and none of it is fussy. It is here so that the cottages stay the quiet places you booked them for — for you, and for whoever comes up the hill next.

Booking a cottage means agreeing to these rules, and to our booking policies. If anything here would be difficult for your group, tell us before you book and we will say honestly whether it can be worked around.

01

Arrival & departure

Check-in
2:00 pm – 7:00 pm. Arriving later is fine if you tell us in advance — the road up is dark and we would rather meet you than leave you looking for the gate.
Check-out
11:00 am. Later check-out is sometimes possible when no one is arriving the same day; ask us the evening before rather than on the morning.
Early arrival
We need the morning to turn the cottage around. If you reach Galiyat early, leave your bags with us and go for a walk.
Minimum stay
Two nights, all year.
02

Who is staying

  • Everyone on the booking, and only them. Each cottage sleeps four to six. Tell us the real number when you book — the beds, the linen and the breakfast all follow from it.
  • Bring ID. Pakistani law requires us to record a CNIC or passport for the lead guest. We take the number and nothing else.
  • Day visitors are welcome; overnight ones are not. Friends can come up for the afternoon if you let us know. Anyone sleeping in the cottage has to be on the booking.
  • No parties, shoots or events. The cottages are for staying in. Weddings, mehndis, corporate offsites and commercial photography all need a separate arrangement with us first.
03

Quiet & neighbours

  • Quiet hours are 10:00 pm to 7:00 am. Sound carries a long way across a valley at night. Music outdoors, generators and loud speakers stay off during those hours.
  • Two cottages, one hillside. When both are let, please keep the shared lawn and paths neighbourly — the other guests came for the same silence you did.
  • Drones need our say-so. Ask before you fly one; the neighbouring homes value their privacy.
04

Fire, heat & smoking

  • The fireplace is yours to use. Wood is laid for you and more can be brought up. Use the screen, keep it fed with the wood we provide, and never leave it burning when you go out or go to bed.
  • The fire pit is for the evening, not the night. Put it out fully before you turn in. In dry spells we may ask you not to light it at all — the forest starts a few metres away.
  • No smoking indoors. Smoke on the deck or in the garden and use the ashtrays, not the treeline. Smoking inside means a deep-clean charge.
  • Gas heaters and candles. Turn heaters off before you sleep and never leave a candle burning in an empty room.
  • Fireworks are not allowed anywhere on the property.
05

Children & pets

  • Children are very welcome. It is an old house on a slope, with a fireplace and steps — please keep an eye on small ones, especially around the deck and the fire.
  • Pets by prior arrangement only. Ask when you book. If we say yes, they stay off the beds and sofas, and anything they do outside is yours to clear up.
06

Living on the mountain

  • Water and power are mountain supplies. We have back-up for both, but cuts happen in bad weather. Please use water thoughtfully, especially in summer.
  • The road is a mountain road. In winter it can need a four-wheel drive or chains, and it can close in heavy snow. Check with us a day or two before you set off.
  • Park where we show you. The access track has to stay clear for the other cottage and for emergency vehicles.
  • Food indoors. Monkeys and other wildlife will let themselves in through an open door. Keep food inside and windows shut when you go out.
  • Take your rubbish to the bins we point out — nothing left outside overnight.
07

Looking after the cottage

  • Tell us when something breaks. Accidents happen and we would far rather hear about it than find it. Nothing small is ever charged for.
  • Serious damage or loss is chargeable at the cost of repair or replacement, as is a deep clean after smoking indoors or a party we did not agree to.
  • Shoes off inside is the house habit — the floors are wood and the hill is muddy.
  • Furniture stays where it is. Ask us if you want the room laid out differently and we will help move it.
  • Housekeeping visits through your stay. Tell us what time suits you, or ask us to leave you alone entirely.
08

Leaving

  • No stripping beds, no washing up rotas. Leave the dishes rinsed, the rubbish bagged, and the rest to us.
  • Close the windows, kill the fire, switch off the heaters and hand the keys back to whoever meets you.
  • Anything left behind we will keep for thirty days and post on at your cost if you want it.
A word about the wording

These rules are a draft prepared for the owners of Woodland Cottages and have not yet been approved or checked against Pakistani hospitality regulation. Times, charges and the ID requirement in particular should be confirmed before this page goes live.
