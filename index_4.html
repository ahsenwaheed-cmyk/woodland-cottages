The Place

Small on purpose,
high in the hills.

Woodland Cottages sits among the pines in the Galiyat hills, a few hours north of Islamabad. Mornings are slow here — fog on the treeline, coffee on the veranda, birdsong instead of traffic.

We keep it to two cottages and long, unhurried stays, for families and couples who'd rather have the forest to themselves.
