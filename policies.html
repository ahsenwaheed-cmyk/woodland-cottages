Policies

What happens if your plans change — cancelling, moving your dates, and how money comes back to you. Written to be read once, not decoded later.

These policies apply to bookings made directly with Woodland Cottages — through this website, by WhatsApp, or by phone. They sit alongside our house rules. All times are Pakistan Standard Time, and "days before arrival" means whole days before your check-in date.

01

Cancellation policy

Cancel in writing — a WhatsApp message or an email to hello@woodlandcottage.pk is enough. The refund follows the date we receive it, not the date you decided.

Standard dates

You cancelYou get back
14 or more days before arrival100% of what you paid, less the payment-processing fee
7 to 13 days before arrival50% of the total booking value
Fewer than 7 days, or no arrivalNo refund — though we will always try to re-let the nights, and refund you if we do

Peak dates

Peak dates are both Eid holidays, 20 December to 5 January, and weekends from June through August. They are flagged on the booking calendar when you choose your nights.

You cancelYou get back
30 or more days before arrival100% of what you paid, less the payment-processing fee
14 to 29 days before arrival50% of the total booking value
Fewer than 14 days, or no arrivalNo refund

If we have to cancel

If we cancel your booking for any reason of ours — damage to the cottage, a burst pipe, anything at all — you get every rupee back, and we will help you find somewhere else in Galiyat for the same dates.

Snow, landslides and closed roads

If the road to the property is officially closed, or a government restriction makes the stay impossible, we will not keep your money for a stay you could not take. You may move the booking to any date within twelve months at no charge, or take a full refund. Bad weather that merely makes the drive unpleasant is not the same thing as a closed road, and is treated as an ordinary cancellation.

02

Booking change policy

Plans move. Where we can accommodate it, we will.

  • One free date change. Ask 14 or more days before arrival and we will move your booking once, to any available dates within the next twelve months, with no change fee. If the new dates cost more, you pay the difference; if they cost less, the balance stays as credit against the stay.
  • Changes inside 14 days are treated as a cancellation followed by a new booking, and the cancellation table above applies. Ask anyway — if the nights are easy for us to re-let, we will usually just move you.
  • Swapping cottages — Blue for Green or the other way round — is free whenever the other cottage is open for your dates.
  • Adding nights is free and always welcome, subject to availability at the rate for those nights.
  • Dropping nights 14 or more days out is refunded in full for the nights removed. Inside 14 days, removed nights follow the cancellation table.
  • More guests than you booked for needs our agreement before arrival, and may carry an additional-guest charge. Turning up with more people than the cottage sleeps is not something we can fix on the night.
  • Changing the lead guest is fine — tell us the new name and ID details before arrival.

Every change has to be confirmed by us in writing before it counts. A change you have asked for but we have not confirmed is not yet a change.

03

Refund policy

How it comes back
To the card or account you paid from. We cannot refund to a different account, and we do not refund in cash.
How long
We release approved refunds within 3 working days. Your bank then takes its own time — usually 5 to 10 working days for a card, 3 to 5 for a bank transfer.
What is deducted
Payment-gateway and bank charges are not refundable; they were paid to the processor, not to us. Everything else follows the cancellation table.
Damage deposit
Where one is taken, it is released within 7 days of check-out, less the documented cost of any damage. We send you the photographs and the bill for anything we deduct.

Leaving early

Nights you have booked but do not use are not refunded, whatever the reason for leaving — unless the cottage itself became unusable, in which case we refund the unused nights in full.

Not arriving

If you have not arrived and not contacted us by noon the day after check-in, we treat the booking as cancelled with no refund, and the cottage goes back on the calendar.

If something was wrong

If the cottage is not as described, tell us during your stay and give us the chance to put it right — that is nearly always possible within the hour. If we cannot, we will agree a fair reduction there and then. Complaints raised for the first time after check-out are much harder for either of us to settle honestly.

04

Booked somewhere else?

If you booked through Airbnb, Booking.com or any other platform, that platform's cancellation and refund terms apply, not these, and changes and refunds have to go through them. We cannot refund a payment we never received. Booking with us directly is the cheaper and the more flexible of the two.

Nothing on this page takes away rights you have under Pakistani consumer law.

A word about the wording

These policies are a draft prepared for the owners of Woodland Cottages. The timings, percentages, peak-date windows, deposit handling and refund turnarounds are proposals, not established terms, and none of it has been reviewed by a lawyer. They should be confirmed against how the property actually operates — and against what Smoobu and the listing platforms are set to enforce — before this page is published.
